PLATFORM

What the system does

Six capabilities, each demonstrable in the sections that follow.

01 · ASSESSMENT

Hourly disruption scoring

806 road segments across Sikkim are scored against rainfall, terrain, substrate and closure history, and re-evaluated every hour.

02 · ROUTING

Risk-aware route resolution

Journeys are solved over the road graph with cost weighted by segment risk and vehicle class, so a relief convoy and a heavy goods vehicle receive different answers.

03 · RESPONSE

Automatic re-routing

When a segment becomes restricted mid-journey, the route is recomputed around it and the revised plan is issued with the delay stated explicitly.

04 · ANALYSIS

Network criticality

Bridge detection over the road graph identifies segments whose loss disconnects settlements entirely, and names the settlements affected.

05 · LANGUAGE

Read in your own language

Reports and advisories are shown in the reader's language, with Hindi, Nepali and Bengali checked by hand. Where no verified translation exists the English source is shown unchanged rather than machine-guessed.

06 · VERIFICATION

Reports from the public, checked

Anyone can report a blocked or damaged road. A report is held as unconfirmed until a second, independent one agrees with it, so one mistaken message cannot move a road's score on its own.
